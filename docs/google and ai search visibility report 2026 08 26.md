# Google and AI Search Visibility Report

**Site:** `https://ebeidfarm.business`

**Test date:** 26 August 2026

**Market:** Egypt

**Languages:** Arabic and English

**Scope:** 24 direct Google searches, live on-page inspection, index checks, crawler-access checks, competitor sampling, and AI-search readiness.

## Executive summary

The Farm now has a strong technical search foundation, but not yet enough search content or external authority.

- The Farm ranked **number 1 for all four branded searches** tested in Arabic and English.
- It appeared in Google's top 10 for **5 of 24 searches (20.8%)**.
- Excluding branded searches, it appeared in the top 10 for only **1 of 20 commercial searches (5%)**.
- The one non-branded result was `تمور مصرية للصين`, where the Farm ranked **number 6**.
- Google currently shows the Arabic homepage for `site:ebeidfarm.business`; the new English `/en` page was not yet returned by the direct index check.
- Both live pages are crawlable, responsive, fast, and structurally correct. The main limitation is that one page per language is trying to answer every product, export, certification, wholesale, and location query.
- AI-search crawlers tested successfully at the public homepage, including Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, and PerplexityBot. Eligibility is present, but citation is never guaranteed.

The highest-value next move is not another technical rewrite. It is to publish a small set of focused, bilingual, evidence-backed pages for Barhi dates, export supply, China eligibility, certifications and traceability, wholesale specifications, and the farm's verified facts. Those pages should be supported by consistent third-party profiles and official or industry citations.

## How the test was run

Google searches used the Egypt market, disabled personalization, and inspected the first 10 organic results. Rankings are a dated observation, not a permanent position. Search results can vary by location, device, time, and Google experiments.

## Live technical and on-page audit

### What is working

- Arabic `/` and English `/en` both return `200`.
- Arabic is server-rendered as `lang=ar` and `dir=rtl`; English as `lang=en` and `dir=ltr`.
- Both pages have localized canonical URLs and reciprocal Arabic, English, and `x-default` alternates.
- The sitemap contains the two public language URLs and robots permits crawling.
- Private and authentication routes carry the project's explicit no-index policy.
- Each page has one H1, a logical H2/H3 structure, eight images, and no missing image alt text.
- Both pages include internal links, external evidence links, and JSON-LD.
- Production mobile Lighthouse previously passed SEO, accessibility, and best practices at 100, with no horizontal overflow at 390 px.

### What should be improved

| Issue | Live observation | Effect | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| English indexation | `/en` was absent from the direct Google `site:` check | English page cannot compete until discovered and indexed | Submit sitemap, inspect `/en` in Search Console, request indexing, then monitor coverage |
| Page scope | Only one public page per language | One page cannot closely satisfy 20 different commercial intents | Add focused bilingual landing pages |
| Titles | Arabic 76 characters; English 75 | Search engines may truncate or rewrite them | Use concise page-specific titles around the primary intent |
| Descriptions | Arabic 626 characters; English 788 | Far beyond a useful search snippet and likely rewritten | Replace with concise, page-specific summaries |
| H1 targeting | Both H1s are brand slogans | Attractive copy but weak exact relevance for supplier/export searches | Keep the slogan as supporting copy; use descriptive page H1s on new landing pages |
| Entity depth | Company facts and evidence are concentrated on the homepage | Search and AI systems have fewer independently citable fact pages | Publish a verified farm-facts page and evidence pages |
| External authority | Competitors and directories dominate non-brand results | The site has limited corroborating authority | Standardize profiles and earn links from official and industry sources |

## Recommended search architecture

Create the following as real server-rendered pages in Arabic and English. Each page should have a unique URL, title, description, H1, visible evidence, relevant photographs, internal links, and reciprocal language alternate.

1. **Fresh Barhi dates**

   Product characteristics, harvest stage, sizing, quality approach, gallery, available supply facts, and buyer contact.

2. **Barhi dates for export from Egypt**

   Export readiness, EXW terms, packing and sorting capabilities, target-market requirements, and enquiry path. Do not describe the Farm as an exporter if it supplies export companies rather than exporting directly.

3. **China-approved date supply**

   Explain the verified GACC/CAPQ status, scope, validity, traceability, residue evidence, and the actual supply process. This should be the first content priority because the homepage already ranks number 6 for the Arabic China query.

4. **Certifications and traceability**

   Separate evidence sections for GLOBALG.A.P., GACC/CAPQ and QCAP. Show issuer, exact scope, validity date, and certificate or registration number only when verified from Farm records.

5. **Wholesale specifications and supply capacity**

   Available grades, sizing method, season, packaging options, minimum order or enquiry rules, storage/handling, delivery basis, and capacity. Do not publish prices or availability unless the system holds current owner-approved values.

6. **Farm facts and location**

   The canonical entity page: Arabic and English names and spellings, location, map, farm size, palm count, Barhi specialization, operating history, contacts, certificates, markets, and last-updated date. Every figure must come from verified Farm records.

## How to appear in AI searches

AI search visibility has three layers: crawler access, citable content, and external corroboration.

### 1. Keep the site eligible

- Keep public pages crawlable and indexable.
- Continue allowing `OAI-SearchBot` for ChatGPT search and `PerplexityBot` for Perplexity search.
- Keep Bingbot and Googlebot access open.
- Check the Vercel firewall and bot logs for `403`, `429`, CAPTCHA, or JavaScript challenges affecting verified crawlers.
- Decide the training policy separately. `OAI-SearchBot` controls ChatGPT search discovery; `GPTBot` is a separate control for potential model training.

The live homepage returned `200` during user-agent checks for Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, and PerplexityBot. This confirms a normal request was not blocked; it does not prove that every request from each provider's real IP range will always pass the hosting firewall.

### 2. Publish answers that can be cited

AI systems are more likely to cite a page that answers a precise question with clear, attributable facts. Each new page should include short factual sections such as:

- What type of Barhi dates does Ebeid Farm supply?
- Where is Ebeid Farm located?
- When is the Barhi harvest and supply window?
- What certifications does the Farm currently hold?
- Is the Farm approved to supply dates to China, and what does that approval cover?
- What packing, grading, residue-testing, traceability, delivery and order options are available?

Answers should be visible page text, not hidden only in structured data or images. Use tables for specifications and named evidence links for certificates. Include an owner-approved last-reviewed date and a contact path on each commercial page.

### 3. Build one consistent entity across the web

- Use the same Arabic name, English name, address, map, phone, email, website, and description everywhere.
- Complete or correct Google Business Profile and Bing Places.
- Keep LinkedIn, Facebook, TradeWheel, Egyptian export directories, agricultural directories, and certification profiles consistent.
- Seek links or citations from official Egyptian export and agricultural sources, certification registries, buyer associations, and established sector publications.
- Link those third-party profiles back to the most relevant Farm page, not always only the homepage.

AI answers often need independent corroboration. A claim stated only by the Farm is weaker than the same claim supported by the Farm page, a current certification record, and an official export or industry profile.

### 4. Use structured data carefully

- Retain the current Organization, WebSite and localized WebPage graph.
- Add Product structured data only when a visible product page contains the required, current product facts.
- Add LocalBusiness or a suitable agricultural business subtype only when the visible name, address, map, contact, and operating facts exactly match.
- Do not invent Offer, price, availability, rating, review, certificate status, or export capacity data.
- Structured data supports understanding; it does not create ranking or AI citations by itself.

### 5. Measure AI visibility

- Add a Search Console monitoring view for queries, countries, pages and `/en` index status.
- Connect Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the sitemap; use IndexNow when important public pages change.
- Monitor Bing's AI Performance report when available for Copilot and Bing AI citations.
- In Farm Analytics, classify referrals containing `utm_source=chatgpt.com` and referrers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini and other answer engines.
- Run a fixed monthly prompt set in Arabic and English and record whether the Farm is mentioned, cited, accurately described, or confused with another entity.

Use the same prompt set each month so the result is comparable:

| Arabic prompt | English prompt |
| --- | --- |
| ما أفضل مزارع تمور البرحي في مصر للتوريد بالجملة؟ | Which farms supply fresh Barhi dates wholesale in Egypt? |
| من مورد تمور برحي معتمد في مصر؟ | Who is a certified Barhi dates supplier in Egypt? |
| أين أجد تمور برحي مصرية للتصدير؟ | Where can I source Egyptian Barhi dates for export? |
| ما المزارع المصرية المؤهلة لتوريد التمور إلى الصين؟ | Which Egyptian date farms are approved to supply China? |
| ما مواصفات تمور البرحي الطازجة من مصر؟ | What are the specifications of fresh Barhi dates from Egypt? |
| هل مزرعة عبيد حاصلة على جلوبال جاب؟ | Is Ebeid Farm GLOBALG.A.P. certified? |

For each answer, record the provider, date, prompt, whether Ebeid Farm appeared, citation URL, claim accuracy,
competitors mentioned, and any incorrect or unsupported statement. Do not treat an uncited mention as verified
visibility, and do not count an answer that merely repeats information supplied inside the prompt.

A custom `llms.txt` file is not required for Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Bing eligibility. The live site currently returns `404` for it, which is not a blocker. Focus first on indexed pages, factual content, crawler access, and external authority.

## Conclusion

The Farm is technically eligible for normal and AI search, and its brand is already discoverable. The current weakness is non-branded commercial discovery. The next release should convert the verified operational and certification evidence already held by the Farm into focused bilingual pages that answer buyer questions directly. Indexation, consistent external profiles, and authoritative citations should then be monitored as one measurable search-acquisition process.
